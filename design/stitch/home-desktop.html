Hampi Boulders & Sunset Ferry Logistics

“Rent a bicycle at sunrise in Hampi before the granite boulders absorb the noon heat. Cross the river on the public passenger boat before 5:30 PM — after dusk, prices quadruple for private coracles.”

SG
Sharath Gowda Local Field Guide • Hospet & Anegundi

Cloud Inversions at Dawn
