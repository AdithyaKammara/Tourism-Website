Ziro is a high, wide valley in Arunachal Pradesh and home to the Apatani people. They grow rice and raise fish in the same flooded fields, a sustainable system good enough to put the valley on UNESCO's tentative World Heritage list.

## What to do

- **Walk between the villages** (Hong, Hari, Bulla) through the paddies, ideally with a local guide who can introduce you.
- **The Ziro Festival of Music**, usually held in late September, is one of India's best-loved independent music festivals.
- **Visit during the harvest** in September and October, when the fields turn gold.

## Good to know

Indian visitors need an Inner Line Permit (ILP) to enter Arunachal Pradesh; you can apply online. Foreign visitors need a Protected Area Permit. Evenings are cold for most of the year, so pack layers.
