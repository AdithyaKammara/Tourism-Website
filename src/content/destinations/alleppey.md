Alappuzha (most people still say Alleppey) is the gateway to Kerala's backwaters: a maze of lakes, canals and rivers lined with paddy fields and coconut palms. Life here happens on the water. Kids go to school by boat and the post office has a jetty.

## What to do

- **Stay a night on a houseboat.** The old rice barges (kettuvallam) now carry two or three bedrooms and a cook. Book through a licensed operator and agree the route before you board.
- **Take the public ferry** to Kottayam for a few rupees. It's the same scenery as a houseboat, with commuters instead of tourists.
- **Rent a canoe** for the narrow canals that big boats can't reach.

## Good to know

The Nehru Trophy Boat Race on Punnamada Lake, held on the second Saturday of August, draws huge crowds to watch hundred-oar snake boats race. Houseboats must moor by early evening, so overnight trips spend the night tied up rather than cruising.
