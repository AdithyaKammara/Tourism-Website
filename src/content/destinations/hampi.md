Hampi was the capital of the Vijayanagara Empire, one of the richest cities in the world in the early 1500s, until it was sacked in 1565. What's left is a UNESCO World Heritage Site spread over dozens of square kilometres: temples, bazaars, stepwells and royal enclosures among granite boulders the size of houses.

## What to do

- **Virupaksha Temple** is still in use, and its tall gateway tower is the landmark you'll navigate by.
- **The Vittala Temple** holds the famous stone chariot and the "musical" pillars.
- **Watch sunset from Hemakuta or Matanga Hill.** Bring a torch for the walk down.
- **Rent a bicycle or e-bike.** The sites are far apart and the roads are mostly flat.

## Good to know

From March to May temperatures regularly pass 40°C, so start early and rest at midday. Hampi is a sacred town: dress modestly and expect alcohol and meat to be hard to find.
