Jaisalmer is called the Golden City because almost everything, from the fort to the merchants' mansions, is built from the same honey-coloured sandstone. The fort dates from the twelfth century, and a few thousand people still live and run shops inside its walls. It's part of the Hill Forts of Rajasthan UNESCO World Heritage Site.

## What to do

- **Walk the fort lanes** early in the morning, before the tour groups arrive.
- **The havelis** (merchants' mansions), especially Patwon ki Haveli, have carved stone facades that look like lace.
- **Spend a night in the desert.** The Sam dunes are around 40 km out and popular. Quieter camps further away mean fewer jeeps and more stars.

## Good to know

Summer is brutally hot, often well over 40°C. The Desert Festival takes place in January or February, depending on the lunar calendar.
