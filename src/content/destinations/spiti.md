Spiti sits in the rain shadow of the Himalaya, so it's high, dry and almost treeless. The main town, Kaza, is around 3,800 metres up. Villages are clusters of whitewashed houses next to barley fields, and Tibetan Buddhist monasteries cling to the ridges above them.

## What to do

- **Key Monastery**, about a thousand years old, looks like it's been stacked on its hill. The monks often invite visitors in for butter tea.
- **Langza, Hikkim and Komic** are among the highest villages reachable by road. Hikkim's post office is a favourite for sending postcards home.
- **Chandratal Lake**, on the way out towards Manali, is worth a night under the stars, if you're properly acclimatised.

## Good to know

Altitude sickness is real here. Go up gradually, spend your first night lower down in Kinnaur, and drink lots of water. ATMs and mobile signal are patchy outside Kaza, so carry cash.
